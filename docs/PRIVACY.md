# Knode - privacy

- Knode keeps your spaces on your devices. Nothing leaves them unless you turn on sync.
- Knode sends no telemetry and no analytics.
- Sync through iCloud stores your changes in your own iCloud. Sync through Knode Cloud is encrypted end
  to end: the server stores data it cannot read.
- Agents only see the spaces you allow, and only write where you let them.
